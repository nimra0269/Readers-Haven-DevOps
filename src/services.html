HERE TO HELP

Make the most of your library.

Drop in during open hours or ask the front desk if you need a hand getting started.

⌕
01 / FIND A BOOK

Online catalogue

Search titles, authors, and subjects using the catalogue at the library desk. Ask staff to check availability or help you find the right shelf.

Available during library hours · Mon–Sat, 9:00 am–8:00 pm; Sun, 10:00 am–5:00 pm
↗
▧
02 / READ ANYWHERE

E-books & digital reading

Members can ask the front desk about the library’s e-book collection and supported borrowing options. Bring your membership card to get started.

Help available at the desk during open hours
↗
⌂
03 / MAKE SOME SPACE

Study-room booking

Reserve a quiet room for up to two hours per visit. Book at the front desk on the day you need it; rooms are subject to availability.

Bookings: 9:00 am–6:00 pm · Same-day reservations
↗
▤
04 / GET IT ON PAPER

Printing & scanning

Bring your document on a USB drive or ask staff for help. Printing is PKR 10 per black-and-white page or PKR 30 per colour page; scanning is PKR 20 per document.

Service desk: Mon–Sat, 9:00 am–7:00 pm
↗
⌁
05 / GET CONNECTED

Free Wi-Fi

Connect your device to the Readers-Haven-Guest network and ask the front desk for that day’s access code. Please keep calls and audio on headphones.

Available in public areas during open hours
↗
